The 10-Point Marketing Growth Checklist

Work through these ten checkpoints to find the fastest wins in your current marketing before changing anything else. Each one takes a few minutes to check.

01 Can a stranger explain what you sell in one sentence?

If your homepage headline needs a follow-up question to make sense, your positioning is doing the opposite of its job.

02 Does every page have one clear next step?

Pages with three competing calls to action usually convert on none of them. Pick the one action that matters most per page.

03 Do you know your top three traffic sources by conversion rate, not just volume?

Volume tells you what's popular. Conversion rate tells you what's actually working — track both.

04 Is your fastest-loading page also your highest-intent page?

Speed matters most where buying decisions happen. Audit load time on pricing, signup, and checkout pages first.

05 Can visitors find your value proposition without scrolling?

Above-the-fold content sets expectations for everything below it — make it count.

06 Are your forms asking for the minimum information you actually need?

Every additional form field is a small tax on conversion. Cut anything you don't act on within the first week.

07 Do you have a documented ideal customer profile, or just a general sense of it?

Written-down specifics (industry, size, role, trigger event) make every other marketing decision faster and sharper.

08 Is your content mapped to a specific stage of the buyer journey?

Awareness content, consideration content, and decision content ask for different things — mixing them up dilutes all three.

09 Do you review campaign performance on a fixed cadence, or only when something goes wrong?

A scheduled monthly (or weekly) review catches slow declines that reactive check-ins miss entirely.

10 Could someone new on your team find your current strategy in writing?

If the strategy only lives in one person's head, it isn't a strategy yet — it's a plan waiting to be written down.
